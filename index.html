About these figures

Taxes are not earmarked for particular services. The government pools what it raises and decides spending separately. In 2025-26 it also borrowed to cover part of what it spent, so taxes paid for less than all of it. This chart shares your tax in the same proportions as the Treasury's total spending.

The figures are the Treasury's total expenditure on services for 2025-26: £1,227.6 billion spent on public services, benefits and debt interest. Outturn means what was actually spent, as opposed to plans. The total leaves out £132.5 billion of accounting adjustments, mostly depreciation and VAT that public bodies pay and get back, which take it to £1,360.1 billion. HMRC's Annual Tax Summaries use the same basis.

Spending is grouped by purpose, using the UN's Classification of the Functions of Government (COFOG), whichever department or council spends it. The methodology explains each category.

Spending: HM Treasury, Public Expenditure Statistical Analyses 2026, Table 5.2 (2025-26 outturn).
